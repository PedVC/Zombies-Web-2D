## 🇬🇧 English

A 2D top-down zombie survival game that runs in the browser, made with
**HTML, CSS and JavaScript** (Canvas). Survive wave after wave, buy perks,
upgrade your weapon and try to last as many rounds as possible.

> **Free to play and share.** You are welcome to play, download and share the
> game. Modifying the game or this repository is not allowed. See
> [Copyright and permissions](#copyright-and-permissions).

### Play

Play online in your browser: **[add your GitHub Pages link here]**

Or download the files and open `index.html` in your browser. No installation
needed.

### The game

Zombies arrive in rounds. Each round brings more and tougher enemies, and
you earn points for every hit, every kill and every round survived. Spend your
points on perks and ammo to stay alive.

### Features

- Round-based survival with progressive difficulty
- 1 or 2 players on the same keyboard
- 3 maps: Abandoned Facility, Ruined Streets and Underground Laboratory
- Perks to buy: Regeneration, Shield, Speed and Weapon+ (up to level 3)
- Ammo crates and reloading
- Accessibility: high-contrast mode and colour palettes for protanopia,
  deuteranopia and tritanopia
- Pause menu and end-of-game summary
- No real-money purchases and no external services: everything runs in your browser

### Controls

| Action | Player 1 | Player 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Move | W A S D | Arrow keys |
| Aim | Mouse | Automatic (nearest zombie) |
| Shoot | Left mouse button | P |
| Reload | R | O |
| Buy / interact | E | E |
| Pause | Esc or Space | Esc or Space |

### Technology

- HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript (no libraries or frameworks)
- HTML Canvas 2D

Joga online no navegador: **[adiciona aqui o teu link do GitHub Pages]**

### O jogo

Os zombies chegam em rondas. Cada ronda traz mais inimigos e mais resistentes,
e ganhas pontos por cada acerto, cada kill e cada ronda sobrevivida. Gasta os
pontos em perks e munição para te manteres vivo.

### Características

- Sobrevivência por rondas com dificuldade progressiva
- 1 ou 2 jogadores no mesmo teclado
- 3 mapas: Instalação Abandonada, Ruas em Ruínas e Laboratório Subterrâneo
- Perks para comprar: Regeneração, Escudo, Velocidade e Arma+ (até ao nível 3)
- Caixas de munição e recarregamento
- Acessibilidade: modo de alto contraste e paletas de cores para protanopia,
  deuteranopia e tritanopia
- Menu de pausa e resumo no fim do jogo
- Sem compras com dinheiro real e sem serviços externos: tudo corre no teu navegador
